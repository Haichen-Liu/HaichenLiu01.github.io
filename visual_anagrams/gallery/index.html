Image Gallery — all 350 individual generations

Browse every individual generation from the LAVA main ablation, organised by prompt case. Pixel baseline: 7 cases × 5 seeds × 2 views = 70 PNGs. Latent main suite: 7 cases × 4 methods × 5 seeds × 2 views = 280 PNGs. Click any thumbnail on a per-case page to open the full-resolution single image.

How to read this gallery. Pixel images come from the DeepFloyd IF (T5 text encoder) run on Colab and serve as a single-regime sanity reference. Latent images come from the LAVA pipeline on Stable Diffusion v1.5 (CLIP ViT-L/14 text encoder) for all four ablation methods at five fixed seeds. Because the two regimes use different text encoders, cross-regime CLIP-based numbers are not directly comparable; the formal quantitative claim is the n=35 paired latent ablation reported on the project page.
