inner_circle

Transform mechanism (latent side): Inner circle of radius 24/64 rotated 180 degrees; outer annulus stays (mask + 180 rot, both exact). Geng's repo implements this as a true pixel permutation; ours is mask + torch.rot90, which is also interpolation-free and behaves equivalently for our scale.

Exploratory only. Even when the latent-side tensor operation is exact (e.g.\ a pixel or patch permutation), the decoded image-space meaning is not guaranteed: the VAE encoder-decoder is only approximately equivariant under these spatial / sign operations, so D(v_lat(z_t)) is generally not equal to v_img(D(z_t)). Outputs may be visually interesting but do not constitute formal evidence; the formal LAVA experiment is restricted to grid-preserving transforms (rotations and flips).
