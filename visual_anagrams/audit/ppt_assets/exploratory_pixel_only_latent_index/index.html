Exploratory: pixel-only transforms forced into latent

Sandbox probe. Seven transforms documented as not latent-equivariant are forced through the LAVA pipeline at all five fixed seeds under both Equal and LAVA Reverse weighting. Each per-transform page shows the full 5-seed grid with View A (canonical reading) and View B (transformed reading) side by side per cell, mirroring the village_portrait gallery layout used elsewhere on the site.

Exploratory only. These transforms break the latent-equivariance assumption documented in Geng et al. (CVPR 2024, §3.5). Outputs may be visually interesting but do not constitute formal evidence. The formal LAVA experiment is deliberately restricted to grid-preserving transforms (rotations and flips).

Click any card below to open the full per-transform page: 5 fixed seeds × 2 weight conditions, each cell shows View A and View B side by side.

negate

Elementwise sign flip on the latent tensor. Self-inverse but destroys the Gaussian-noise scale assumption used by the scheduler.
skew

Affine shear by 15 degrees with bilinear interpolation. Inverse is the negative shear; non-exact for finite grids.
pixel_permute

Random permutation of all 64x64 = 4096 spatial positions, shared across channels. Permutation indices fixed by seed 12345.
patch_permute_8x8

8x8 grid of 8x8 patches randomly permuted as units. Permutation indices fixed by seed 23456.
inner_circle

Inner circle of radius 24/64 rotated 180 degrees; outer annulus stays. The 180-degree rotation makes this self-inverse on the mask.
square_hinge_tophalf

Top half of the latent (rows 0..31) rotated 180 degrees; bottom half stays. Self-inverse.
jigsaw_4x4

4x4 grid of 16x16 patches randomly permuted (different seed from patch_permute_8x8: 54321). 16 jigsaw pieces.
