Exploratory: pixel-only transforms forced into latent

Exploratory only. These transforms break the latent-equivariance assumption documented in Geng et al. (CVPR 2024, Section 3.5). Outputs may be visually interesting but do not constitute formal evidence. The formal LAVA experiment is restricted to grid-preserving transforms (rotations and flips).
